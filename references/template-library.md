# Promofy HTML template library

Use these historical campaign sources for layout, copy structure, and implementation. Their dates, offers, booking links, tracking, and hosted artwork belong to the original campaign. Confirm current details and test links and assets before reuse. When an email template contains an editing placeholder, replace it in the working copy. Preserve native Brevo variables.

## Product announcements, trials, and editorial

| Campaign | HTML source | What to take from it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI Suite launch | [Launch source](../assets/templates/ai-suite/ai-suite-launch-source.html) | Dark violet hero, five add-on modules, a workflow visual, and a repeated demo action. For a blended hero follow the production guide’s desktop and mobile art direction. |

The two AI Suite files are the HTML attachments recovered from the launch and color-review chats. Some templates use remotely hosted artwork in their source, so an internet connection may be needed to display all images. The SBC team image used by the local previews is included.
