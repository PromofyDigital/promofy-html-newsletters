THE PROMOFY ENGAGEMENT ECOSYSTEM

TURN MORE OF THE CUSTOMER JOURNEY ON

Promofy has evolved beyond individual gamification mechanics into a connected engagement ecosystem designed to support different moments across the customer lifecycle.

At SBC, we’ll be exploring how operators can:

Turn participation on

through interactive campaigns, predictions, challenges and gamified experiences.

 

Turn excitement on

with configurable jackpot experiences built around meaningful player moments.

 

Turn loyalty on

Turn intelligence on

Turn scale on

The goal isn’t to add another disconnected campaign tool. It’s to create a more connected engagement layer that works alongside the technology operators already use.
